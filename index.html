Human Oversight and Accountability for Autonomous AICapability does not equal permission.

As autonomous AI moves from recommendation to consequential execution, accountability depends on more than model behavior. It requires a clear distinction between what a system can do and what it is authorized to do.

Independent research initiative

Independent work examining oversight, authorization integrity and accountability for civilian autonomous AI systems. This site describes research goals and public-interest relevance without disclosing implementation details.

06 / Research lead

Independent research by Florida Keys Life.

William Gehrkens — Research Lead and Inventor, Florida Keys Life

William Gehrkens is the research lead and inventor. The initiative is submitted through Florida Keys Life, a sole proprietorship.

His interest in engineering and computing began early. His father, George R. Gehrkens, was an aeronautical engineer with North American Aviation and introduced William to computers and programming at a young age. Growing up around engineering, aviation and technical problem-solving helped shape a lifelong interest in how complex systems are designed, controlled and made accountable.

That perspective now informs his independent research into meaningful human oversight, authorization integrity and accountability as artificial intelligence systems become increasingly capable of autonomous action.

Patent pending. William Gehrkens is the inventor named in a U.S. provisional patent application relating to this research.
